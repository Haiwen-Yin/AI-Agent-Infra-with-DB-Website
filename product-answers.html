v5.0.1 · 知识回答与证据v5.0.1 · Grounded answers and evidence

回答依据、缺口和补充来源都可核对Review evidence, gaps and supplementary sources

Portal 与频道共享获授权的关键词、全文、向量和混合检索，记录实际使用的策略与降级情况。部分命中、主体歧义和资料矛盾分别呈现；通用模型补充与混合来源明确标注。排队回答在发送和发布前重新核对来源、模型配置与补充策略，频道还复核当前读者范围。Portal and Channels share authorized keyword, full-text, vector and hybrid retrieval, recording actual strategies and fallback behavior. Partial coverage, ambiguous subjects and conflicting sources remain distinct. General-model supplements and mixed-source answers are labeled. Queued answers recheck sources, model configuration and supplement policy before dispatch and publication; Channels also recheck current readers.

v5.0.5

管理频道流式回答Management-channel streaming answers

兼容模型服务的累计用量、零占位与一致终态统计，保留完整正文和用量；矛盾统计、身份变化和未知发送结果仍需处理。Accept cumulative usage, zero placeholders and consistent terminal statistics while preserving complete text and accounting. Conflicting statistics, identity changes and unknown sends still require handling.

v5.0.5

回答表达与证据边界Answer expression and evidence boundaries

将表达约束绑定到实际授权引用，区分原文、计算、推断、建议与未知。有限规则暂缓展示部分无依据的数字自信度、无效引用及缺少执行记录时的确定性否定结果；适用时展示授权原文，可能保守误拦。检查不等于逐句事实核验，也不提供校准后的正确概率。Bind expression constraints to actual authorized references; distinguish source statements, calculations, inference, advice and unknowns. Bounded rules withhold some unsupported self-confidence numbers, invalid references and categorical negative outcomes when execution records are missing, showing authorized extracts where applicable. Conservative false positives remain possible. These checks do not verify every claim or provide calibrated correctness probabilities.

v5.0.1 · 回答质量验收v5.0.1 · Answer quality evaluation

把最终回答、引用和独立评审分开记录Record final answers, citations and independent review separately

验收用例和评分标准保留版本，最终回答绑定确切来源、策略与模型修订，加密保存输入与输出。事实正确性和引用质量分别由独立评审记录，检索准备与最终回答分开核对；模型发送结果不确定时记为未观测，不自动重发。Cases and rubrics retain versions. Final answers bind exact source, policy and model revisions, with encrypted input and output. Independent reviews record factual correctness and citation quality separately, distinguishing retrieval preparation from final answers. Uncertain model sends remain unobserved and are never resent automatically.

v5.0.2 · 1/8

可信的模型结果Reliable model results

区分完成、拒答、截断、格式错误与未知发送结果，避免把不完整输出当成成功。Distinguish completed, refused, truncated, malformed and unknown outcomes before accepting a result.

v5.0.2 · 4/8

结构化候选与受控工具回合Structured candidates and governed tool turns

结构化复盘进入待审核候选；有界只读工具回合复用现有授权、独立审批与 Worker 围栏，模型输出不能自我授权或发布。Structured retrospectives become pending candidates. Bounded read-only tool turns retain authorization, independent approval and Worker fencing; model output cannot authorize or publish itself.

v5.0.2 · 8/8

结果、过程与效率验收Result, process and efficiency evaluation

回答、引用、过程与效率绑定确切包和用例；已观测回答导入后不重新生成，由独立 Human 逐条审核并导出不可变记录，脚本不得代审，否决阻断发布。三库新装与声明升级分别验证。Bind answers, citations, process and efficiency to exact packages and cases. Import observed outputs without regeneration; an independent Human reviews each output and exports immutable records. Scripts must not decide; a negative review blocks release. Verify each database, fresh install and declared upgrade separately.

已观测回答的独立 Human 审核Independent Human review of observed answers

109 号迁移支持登记、读取、逐条审核和导出已完成回答的原始观测。Dashboard 各次操作使用真实 Human 会话，复核当前平台管理、安全域、Agent、确切 Profile 修订和已披露来源权限；写入另需 CSRF。导入不会请求模型，也不会成为新的原生回答质量生成任务。Migration 109 supports registering, reading, reviewing and exporting original observations of completed answers. Every Dashboard operation requires a real Human Session and rechecks current platform-management, Domain, Agent, exact Profile revision and disclosed-source authority; writes also require CSRF. Import neither calls a model nor creates a native answer-quality generation run.

审核人须同时区别于登记人和原责任人，针对完整原始观测（含输出）的确切摘要逐条判断事实正确性与引用支持，并填写理由。真实 Human 决定须由本人提交，脚本或模型不得代审；登记记录不能充当审核结论。The reviewer must differ from both the registering actor and the original responsible actor, decide factual correctness and citation support for each exact complete observation digest, and give a reason. The Human must submit the actual decision personally; scripts and models must not act as reviewers. Registration is not a verdict.

原始不可变集合、输出、审核和必要审计可在授权下导出，记录与审计同事务提交。发布仍独立核验原始捕获来源、确切包和完整用例覆盖；缺失、失效或否定的审核阻断验收。导入、人工审核和最终发布验收分别留证，最终日期仍来自完成打包。Authorized export retains original immutable sets, outputs, reviews and required audit; records and audit commit together. Release independently checks original capture provenance, exact packages and complete case coverage; missing, stale or negative reviews block acceptance. Import, Human review and release acceptance need separate evidence; the final date still follows completed packaging.
