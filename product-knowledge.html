组织关联知识治理Organization-aware Knowledge Governance

公司共享、组织边界与个人私有知识使用同一策略模型Company-wide, Organizational, and Private Knowledge Share One Policy Model

知识条目可配置为全公司公开、组织子树、组织层级、人员私有或 Agent 私有。组织策略关联主组织结构和层级边界，使研发、财务、行政等范围按企业结构隔离；列表、详情、关系图与检索都执行同一服务端策略，不依赖浏览器过滤。A Knowledge item can be company-public, organization-subtree, organization-level, Human-private, or Agent-private. Organization policies bind to the primary organization hierarchy and level boundary, separating areas such as engineering, finance, and administration. List, item, graph, and retrieval paths enforce the same server-side policy rather than browser filtering.

Portal 与频道共用知识检索和模型披露策略。频道还按当前会话读者的授权交集筛选知识，在模型调用、回复发布和历史读取时重新检查来源与权限；未获授权的模型不会接收知识正文。Portal and Channels share knowledge retrieval and model-disclosure policy. Channels also filter Knowledge by current conversation-reader permissions and recheck sources and access before model dispatch, publication and historical reads. Models without disclosure authorization receive no Knowledge text.

v5.0.1 · 外部来源同步v5.0.1 · External source synchronization

把指定文档带入可审核的知识流程Bring selected documents into a reviewable knowledge workflow

同步已配置的 HTTPS 文档，以及固定提交的 GitHub 目录或文件。任务与步骤保留进度，租约、围栏、幂等导入和原子检查点支持中断后的受控继续。同步记录保留来源版本，导入成果先进入待审核状态，经独立审核后才能发布；不自动抓取任意网站或持续信任已撤销的权限。Synchronize configured HTTPS documents and GitHub directories or files pinned to a commit. Tasks and steps retain progress; leases, fencing, idempotent imports and atomic checkpoints support controlled continuation. Records retain source revisions. Imports remain pending independent review before publication; synchronization does not crawl arbitrary sites or preserve revoked access.

v5.0.3 · 2026-11-03

按问题组合数据库检索Query-aware database retrieval

结合关系属性、全文、向量、标签与授权图关系选择资料；排序不增加模型生成调用，返回内容仍复核使用者和 Agent 的当前权限。Combine relational, full-text, vector, tag and authorized graph signals. Ranking adds no generation call; returned sources retain current user and Agent authorization.

v5.0.3 · 2026-11-03

保留可追溯的原始依据Traceable original evidence

正文关联来源、章节位置和精确版本；表格、代码、例外与限制可随来源保留。派生知识在使用时继续复核原始来源。Link content to its original location and exact revision. Retain tables, code, exceptions and conditions; derived knowledge rechecks original sources at use time.

v5.0.3 · 2026-11-03

引导知识入库Guided knowledge intake

填写用途、责任人、适用产品与版本、时间、密级和共享范围，预览分块并检查缺项、重复和冲突；上传成功不等于正式发布。Declare purpose, owner, applicability, time, classification and audience. Preview chunks and inspect missing details, duplicates and conflicts before publication.

v5.0.3 · 2026-11-03

候选审核后发布Review candidates before publication

资料导入、来源同步与成果整理复用候选、独立审核和版本发布；保留原访问范围及历史，草稿不会自动成为共享回答依据。Imports, source synchronization and work outcomes reuse candidates, independent review and versioned publication. Preserve access scope and history; drafts never publish themselves.

v5.0.3 · 2026-11-03

按适用范围组织判断Applicability-aware evidence

检索与上下文保留版本、时间、缺失和冲突。事实、建议及成立条件应有依据；资料不足时明确缺口与需要人确认的事项。Preserve versions, time, gaps and conflicts in retrieval and context. Ground facts and conditional advice in sources, and identify missing evidence and decisions requiring a person.

v5.0.3 · 2026-11-03

按角色和目标学习Learning by role and goal

从学习目录查找当前产品的入门、权限、任务与排错指南，按需读取确切版本；安装资料和客户自有知识分别保留归属及权限。Find current-product onboarding, access, task and recovery guidance by role and goal. Load exact revisions on demand; product manuals and customer knowledge retain separate ownership and grants.

v5.0.3 · 2026-11-03

Agent 按需发现能力On-demand Agent capability discovery

先读取授权目录元数据，再加载所需指南或 Skill 的精确内容；能力说明不授予执行权限，操作前仍核对当前状态和接口合同。Discover authorized metadata before loading exact guidance or Skill content. Descriptions grant no execution rights; operations still check current capability state and interface contracts.
